The Mission

Information shouldn't have a language barrier

The ecosystem was built around a simple but ambitious goal: enable people across India to access information, services, and digital experiences in the language they understand best. That created a design challenge most teams never have to face — how do you design for users who don't necessarily think, read, search, or communicate in English?

The Design Challenge

Five things that made this harder than a typical product problem

Personal

Language is personal

It isn't a setting. It shapes how someone searches, understands, and navigates.

Diversity

No single language experience

Different scripts, literacy levels, cultural contexts, and digital familiarity — all at once.

Scale

Information is everywhere

Official content, documents, audio, visual information — all needed to become accessible.

Trust

AI shouldn't be another barrier

Powerful language technology had to feel simple enough for everyday, non-technical use.

System

Many experiences, one system

Mobile, web, audio, visual, and document surfaces needed to feel like one relationship.

My Design Question

How might we make digital information feel native to every Indian language user?

Native, not translated

Design around the user's language rather than treating it as an afterthought.

Simple, not technical

Hide AI complexity behind interactions anyone can understand.

Inclusive by default

Design for different abilities, literacy levels, and digital familiarity.

Consistent, not uniform

Shared patterns that still let different languages and scripts breathe.

Accessible across mediums

Text, voice, visual, and audio — all first-class, none an afterthought.

Outcomes

From fragmented experiences to an ecosystem

More consistent multilingual interaction patterns across five previously disconnected products.

A reusable design-system foundation, adopted by teams that had never shared one before.

Stronger, standardised accessibility practices instead of a per-team judgment call.

AI workflows simplified into interactions a non-technical user could follow unassisted.

Clearer working language between design, product, AI, and engineering teams.

Experiences designed around native-language access, not translated as an afterthought.

✦ Key Reflection
"Language isn't a UI setting — it's part of identity, context, and human access."
Core Principle

Good AI makes technology capable. Good design makes it accessible.
